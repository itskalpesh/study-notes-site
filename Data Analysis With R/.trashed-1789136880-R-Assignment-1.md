1. Explain classification of data.
- Nature of data refers to the type, form and characteristics of data.
- It describes how data is collected, organised, stored and used for analysis.
     1. By Source of data 
     - Data is classified based on where it is collected from.
        1. Primary Data
        -  Data collected first hand by the researchers for specific purpose.
        - It is original, reliable and more accurate. But it time consuming & costly.
        - Ex : Surveys, interviews, observations, Questionnaires, and experiments.
        
        2. Secondary Data
        -  Data already collected and published by others.
        - It is used for further analysis, save time and cost.
        - Ex : Government report,  Census data, Books, Research papers, website.
    
    2. By Nature of data 
    - Data is classified based on its characteristics 
        1. Qualitative data
        -  Descriptive and non numberical information that represents qualities or characteristics.
        - It cannot be measured using numbers.
        - Ex : Gender, Colour, Blood group, customer feedback.
        
        2. Quantitative data
        -  Numerical information that can be measured or counted. Which used for mathematical and statistical analysis.
        - Ex : Age, Weight, Salary, Mark's 
    
    3. By Measurement 
    - Data is classified based on how it is measured.
        1. Distributed Data
         -  Data that contains countable values.
         - Usually represented as whole numbers, cannot have decimal values.
         - Ex : number of cars, number of books.
        
        2. Continuous Data
        -  Data that can be measured. It may include decimal values within a range.
        -  Ex : Height, Tempreture, Distance.
        
    4. By Structural Format 
    - Data is categorised based on how data is formatted, organized, processed and stored.
        1. Structured data
        - Information organized into a predefined format such as rows and columns.
        - Eassy to search, manage and analyze.
        - Stored in relational databases.
        - Ex : Student database, Bank transaction, Employee details.

        1. Unstructured data 
        - Raw information that does not follow a fixed format.
        - It complex and difficult to Store and analyze.
        - It text, images, videos, and audio.
        - Ex : Social media post, emails, audio & video files.
